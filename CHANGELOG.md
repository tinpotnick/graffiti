## [0.1.0]

First public release.

### Added
- MY TAG (64×64 avatar) and THE WALL (320×180) pixel editor with a 256-colour palette, spray, shapes, mirror mode and PNG import/export.
- Tagging other users' walls, composited on top of the original.
- Markdown text posts with drafts.
- Likes, really-likes and bookmarks; really-likes and bookmarks re-pin content.
- Follow by PeerID or QR code.
- IPFS storage via in-browser Helia, IPNS identity, Pinata pinning and public gateway fallback.
- Desktop (Tauri 2) and Android builds, all Docker-based.
